Return Policy

Your Online Shopping Partner

Important: 48 Hours Return Policy

Customers must submit their return request within 48 hours of product delivery. Requests submitted after 48 hours may not be accepted.

1. Unboxing Video Required

Customers are requested to record a clear video while opening their product parcel.

Please keep the video safely. If you want to request a return, you must send this video as an attachment through email.

There is no video upload option on this website. Send your video directly by email.

2. Reasons for Return

You may request a return for the following reasons:

  • Product received damaged.
  • Wrong product received.
  • Product is defective or not working.
  • Product or parts are missing.
  • Product is significantly different from its description.

3. Details Required for Return

Please include the following details in your email:

  • Customer Name
  • Mobile Number
  • Order ID
  • Order Date
  • Delivery Date
  • Product Name
  • Reason for Return
  • Product Unboxing Video (Email Attachment)

4. How to Submit a Return Request

To request a return, send an email to VIKKA Store within 48 hours of receiving your product.

Mention your order details and reason for return. Attach your product unboxing video to the email.

Our team will review your request and contact you regarding the next steps.
